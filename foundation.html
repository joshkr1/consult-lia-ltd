LIA Care Foundation

Building stronger communities through education, environment, and empowerment

Our Mission

LIA Care Foundation is the philanthropic arm of LIA Consultancy Ltd. We believe businesses have a responsibility to give back. That's why we design and deliver community programs that improve health, protect the environment, and create lasting educational opportunities. Our work is grounded in partnership, sustainability, and measurable impact.

Our Focus Areas

Targeted initiatives that create lasting impact

🎓

Education & Capacity Building

Empowering communities through education, skills development, and knowledge transfer.

  • Vocational training
  • Youth educational support
  • Digital literacy
  • Entrepreneurship development
🌿

Environmental Conservation

Promoting environmental awareness and sustainable practices to protect our planet.

  • Tree planting campaigns
  • Waste management education
  • Community clean-ups
  • Sustainable agriculture training

Community Empowerment

Strengthening communities through health outreach and social empowerment programs.

  • Health outreach
